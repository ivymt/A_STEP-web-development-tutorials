Itm Poutry farm

Founded in 2023, Itm Poultry began with a simple mission: to produce healthy, high-quality poultry products while supporting local food security.

What We Do:

Meat Production/Broiler: We raise top-grade meat birds fed on a highly nutritious, balanced diet.

Our Core Values:

Flock Welfare: Our birds are raised in comfortable, well-ventilated housing with constant access to clean water and optimal lighting.
Community First: We are dedicated to driving local agricultural growth and offering sustainable, healthy food choices.
